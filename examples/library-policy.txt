Northfield Library — sample borrowing policy

Students may borrow up to four books at a time. The standard loan period is 14 calendar days. Students can renew a book once for seven more days if no one else has requested it.

Reference books must stay inside the library. Library members can return books at the front desk or use the return box beside the main entrance.

The library opens at 9:00 a.m. from Monday through Friday. It closes at 6:00 p.m. on those days.
